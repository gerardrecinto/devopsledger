# Go-To-Market

## Positioning

DevOpsLedger is an operational memory layer for platform teams. It turns infra changes into durable decision records: intent, diff, risk, approval, rollback readiness, deployment evidence, incidents, and learning.

## Best First Customer

Platform engineering teams with:

- Terraform/OpenTofu, Argo CD, GitHub, PagerDuty, and Jira.
- Frequent production infrastructure changes.
- Pain reconstructing why changes happened during audits or incidents.
- A desire for self-hosted tooling with no telemetry by default.

## Monetization Path

| Tier | Offer | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| Community Edition | Self-hosted core product, Docker Compose, Helm, core integrations. | Individual platform engineers and small teams. |
| Cloud | Hosted workspaces, managed upgrades, backups, advanced dashboards. | Teams that want convenience. |
| Enterprise | SSO/SAML, SCIM, RBAC, compliance exports, air-gapped bundles, support. | Regulated platform organizations. |
| Services | CI/CD, GitOps, Terraform, risk, and rollback modernization consulting. | Teams with urgent change-management pain. |

## Launch Hooks

1. "Your infrastructure changes deserve memory, not archaeology."
2. "A GitOps change should answer why, who approved it, what changed, how risky it was, and how to roll back."
3. "DevOps dashboards show what is broken. DevOpsLedger remembers why it changed."
